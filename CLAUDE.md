# TwizCrew

A World of Warcraft: Forever addon for the owner and their friends: copies of the addon share information and
trigger effects on each other's screens.

**Read `PROJECT.md` first.** It says what we are making, where we stopped, what an addon can and cannot do in
WoW Forever, and where the game and its AddOns folder are on this PC. Keep it up to date: when a decision is made
or something is learned in the game, write it there (one place for each fact).

We are still **brainstorming**: nothing gets built until the owner has picked the first features and approved a
written description.
